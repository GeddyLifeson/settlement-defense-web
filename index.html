Conquest Map

Control rises while your settlement holds ground -- surviving waves, keeping citizens alive and banking scrap all push the meter. At 100% a region is yours for good and starts shipping scrap to whichever settlement you're currently running. Expanding moves your operation to a fresh settlement in the chosen region; the one you leave keeps its progress.

Research

Research Points accrue passively, scaled by how many citizens are alive -- a settlement that keeps its people alive advances, one that keeps burying them stalls. Shelter, basic defense and scrap power are known from the start and can never be locked away; everything past that is a force multiplier you have to earn. Locked buildables stay visible in the build toolbar, greyed out, so you can see what's coming.
