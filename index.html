Conquest Map

Control rises while your settlement holds ground -- surviving waves, keeping citizens alive and banking scrap all push the meter. At 100% a region is yours for good and starts shipping scrap to whichever settlement you're currently running. Expanding moves your operation to a fresh settlement in the chosen region; the one you leave keeps its progress. Launching an expedition costs scrap up front and carries a real risk of a rough landing; upgrading travel range lets you leapfrog past regions directly adjacent to your holdings.

Research

Research Points accrue passively, scaled by how many citizens are alive -- a settlement that keeps its people alive advances, one that keeps burying them stalls. Shelter, basic defense and scrap power are known from the start and can never be locked away; everything past that is a force multiplier you have to earn. Locked buildables stay visible in the build toolbar, greyed out, so you can see what's coming.

Cliques

Once the settlement grows past 20 living citizens, rival cliques form on their own, each with its own lean (who's more likely to scrap, wreck property, or slip off duty when things go wrong). Satisfy a demand and the clique gets paid off in scrap -- but they'll come back wanting more next time. Ignore one past its deadline and unrest rises, some scrap goes missing, and that clique's own flavor of trouble shows up somewhere in the settlement.

Programs

Skills Workshop, Wellness Counseling, and Community Circle each run in a matching validated room (Training Room / Recreation Room) staffed by a Foreman / Psychologist / Facilitator actually holding the post -- no staffer physically present, no sessions run. Citizens attend voluntarily when they stand to benefit, same as any other need-driven trip. A program lists "Unstaffed" or "No room" until both requirements are met.

Outpost Charter Contracts

Time-Locked Investment
Charter contracts are milestone-gated: the checklist items on a card are free, but the reward only pays out once every item on that card is checked, and the population-tier ladder only unlocks one rung at a time. The Emergency Stabilization Charter only appears once the settlement is genuinely in financial trouble -- and even then it won't pay until the settlement's own finances actually recover, not the moment the crisis is spotted.

Field Contracts

Offered
Active
A Field Contract has a real deadline. Miss it (or lose a citizen mid-Survival contract) and the contract just fails -- the reward is lost, nothing else about the settlement changes. Accepting one is free; declining an offer costs nothing either.

Fabrication

A Fabrication Bay unlocks drone capacity (2 slots per Bay, same "a building unlocks capacity" pattern as an Armory unlocking weapon tiers) -- it doesn't spawn a drone on its own. Fabricating a drone spends scrap immediately and locks it to exactly one work category for its whole lifetime; it rolls off the line after a real gestation delay. A drone has no hunger, rest, social, or mood -- it never eats, sleeps, or breaks, it just works its one assigned job category for as long as work of that kind exists.

Coverage Plans

Each plan is a one-time purchase that permanently discounts its themed buildables and unlocks a matching emergency call-in. A call-in only fires once its real threshold is actually crossed (active fires for Fire Response, downed citizens for Medical Response) -- buying the plan alone does nothing until that threshold is met.
